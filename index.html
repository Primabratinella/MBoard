🇬🇭 GHANA CULTURE SPOTLIGHT

The name Ghana translates historically to "Warrior King." It was the first nation in sub-Saharan Africa to gain independence, earning its title as the "Gateway to Africa." Pray for our partners as they boldly lead communities to the King of Kings!

🌱 LOCAL LIFE & ECONOMY

Ghana is the world's second-largest producer of cocoa beans! Agriculture is the heartbeat of daily life. In the north, Aaron Kabore relies on subsistence farming during the rainy season to sustain his household while holding doors open for the Gospel.

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & DISTRICT LAYOUT

While southern Ghana features thick rainforests and beaches, the Gushego District in the north transitions into sprawling savanna grasslands. It experiences intense rainy seasons followed by harsh, dry seasons.

🌊 GEOGRAPHY SPOTLIGHT

Ghana is home to Lake Volta, the largest artificial man-made lake in the entire world by surface area! It is so massive it can be seen from space. We pray that the knowledge of the Gospel covers the Gushego District just as water covers the lake.
