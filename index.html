🇬🇭 GHANA CULTURE & CHURCH SPOTLIGHT

Known as the "Gateway to Africa," Ghana's name translates to "Warrior King." In the Vittin area, the Good News Bible Church – Tahimayili branch embodies this bold spirit, focusing heavily on vibrant youth mobilization and leadership discipleship.
📌 Pray for the youth of Tahimayili to stand strong as bold ambassadors for Christ!

🌱 NORTHERN LIFE & THE KABORE FAMILY

While southern Ghana is lush with rainforests, the northern Gushego District features sprawling savanna grasslands where subsistence farming is a way of life. Aaron Kabore works the fields during the rainy season for his family's upkeep while leading local cross-cultural Bible studies.
📌 Pray for physical health and a bountiful harvest for Aaron’s family.

🏥 GHANAIAN HOSPITALITY & COMPASSION

Hospitality, or "Akwaaba," is the heartbeat of Ghanaian culture. This same spirit of deep care is seen at the local Christian school, which educates 400 neighborhood children and runs a vital Infants and Children Survival Clinic to protect vulnerable little ones.
📌 Pray for the clinic staff as they extend Christ's love and life-saving care daily.
